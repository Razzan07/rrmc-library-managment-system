About RSMC Library

About Us

Ramswarup Ramsagar Multiple Campus (RRMC) Library is a state-of-the-art academic resource center dedicated to supporting the educational excellence of our students and faculty. Our library houses an extensive collection of books, journals, and digital resources across various disciplines.

Mission & Vision

Mission:

To provide comprehensive information resources and services that support academic learning, research, and intellectual development of our campus community.

Vision:

To be a premier academic library that fosters innovation, collaboration, and lifelong learning in a digital age.

Library Rules & Regulations

  • Be respectful and maintain silence in the library
  • Valid ID card must be presented for borrowing books
  • Each student can borrow maximum 5 books at a time
  • Maximum borrowing period is 14 days
  • Fine for overdue books: Rs. 5 per day per book
  • Food and beverages are not allowed inside
  • Maintain cleanliness and handle books carefully
  • Return damaged or lost books as per library policy
